About these FlowSight resources

This site is published by Manuel Castillejo, creator of FlowSight at flowsight.site. It is an associated resource from the same project.

Ownership and purpose

Guides, templates and project pages serve different reader tasks. They openly identify their common ownership. They are not independent product reviews or third-party endorsements.

The official website remains the source for current features, commercial conditions, documentation and privacy information. These pages provide educational workflows and editable resources.

Sources and examples

Product descriptions use the official FlowSight site and published source. The University of Seville reference is cited only for the recognition it records. Worked examples are labeled fictional, and the app screenshot uses representative demonstration data.

Do not treat patterns of activity as proof of outcome quality, medical diagnoses or comprehensive judgments about a person. Optional connected functions require a review of their actual data flows.

Corrections and reuse

Source and content are available in the linked GitHub repository. Use its issue tracker for a correction with enough detail to identify the page and claim. Avoid including sensitive project material. Editable template text is provided for practical reuse; source-code tooling has its own license.
