The thread through my work

Good software starts with a human question.

My path has moved through games, intergenerational living, AI tools and business workflows. The common thread is turning a real problem into something people can use.

I have worked as a technology lead and from the perspective of business process management. That means understanding the architecture and the people who live with it: their routines, friction and decisions.

FlowSight brings those interests together. I’m building software that helps people understand their work while keeping detailed context close to them. Along the way, I keep making, learning in teams and writing about attention.

My story

A path built by doing.

  1. 2020+

    Software engineering, with a creative instinct

    Software engineering studies at the University of Seville’s ETSII formed the foundation: programming, algorithms, databases and teamwork. Alongside that, I explored 3D and interactive experiences.

    Earlier personal portfolio: KSTY

  2. 2022-2023

    From freelance games to a winning game jam

    I worked on games and 3D assets, then built All The Way UP with Jamongus. We took first place at Scopely Game Jam 2023, an early lesson in shipping with a multidisciplinary team.

    LinkedIn: All The Way UP, Scopely Game Jam 2023Earlier personal portfolio: KSTY

  3. 2024-2025

    Technology leadership meets a social problem

    As CTO at CoNest, I worked on the digital platform for intergenerational living. Fuzzy C-Shell, Pawtel and other projects extended my practice in algorithms, full-stack systems and collaborative product development.

    Earlier personal portfolio: KSTYMancasvel: public repositories and project documentationPawtel: public contributions by Mancasvel

  4. 2025

    AI products, process design and shared wins

    KahuPet won Startup Weekend Sevilla and its cybersecurity recognition. I worked on UNSENT, local AI experiments, study tools and notarial systems, while connecting technical architecture with the realities of people’s work.

    Sevilla Emprendedora: KahuPET gana el XXIX Startup Weekend SevillaLinkedIn: technical leadership and business process managementMancasvel: public repositories and project documentation

  5. 2025

    Building with the entrepreneurship community

    Through Innovators Hub I attended Al-Andalus Innovation Venture and joined NASA Space Apps Challenge with an international team. GALAX brought together education, simulations and space exploration.

    LinkedIn: Innovators Hub and Al-Andalus Innovation VentureLinkedIn: NASA Space Apps Challenge 2025

  6. 2026

    FlowSight, from Seville to Shanghai

    Selected for the spring Xiji Incubator program, I developed FlowSight in Shanghai. In June, the University of Seville recorded the project’s innovative-impact award. The ecosystem now includes desktop, mobile and practical resources.
