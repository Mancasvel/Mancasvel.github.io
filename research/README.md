## Editorial decisions

- Use 2023 for Scopely Game Jam, as explicitly stated in the author's post. Do not confuse it with the separate 2024 event found in general press searches.
- Distinguish NASA Space Apps participation from a NASA job, award, astronaut collaboration or endorsement. Do not reproduce the GALAX prototype's unsupported claims of guidance by real astronauts.
- Xiji resolution: signed 10 November 2025, page 2 names Manuel, program is spring 2026. The LinkedIn post confirms FlowSight development in Shanghai.
- CoNest 2024-2025 and UNSENT 2025 roles come from the earlier personal portfolio. Do not carry forward stale “Now” end dates.
- Describe University of Seville education as studies, not a completed degree. The portfolio also names learning through Lund, Virginia and UNED; no employment relationship or independently verified certificates are claimed.
- No client totals, growth percentages, revenue, user counts, follower counts or claimed productivity improvements. Old portfolio impact figures were not independently supported.
- OIE Labs is linked as an area of practice; no founder title, historical primacy or client metrics are asserted.
- Team projects are credited collectively. KahuPet reporting names six team members; Scopely post names Jamongus teammates.
- The portfolio's Youth Hackathon and ETSII pitch/ad awards were not given enough specific event evidence to promote as headline awards. They remain in the linked earlier portfolio.
- CoNest's old domain did not respond and an earlier Vercel portfolio URL returned 404; prefer repository links.
- Repository creation and update dates do not establish product launch, employment or award dates. Experimental projects are presented without medical efficacy claims.
- Meaningful original projects form the collection; forks, duplicate editions, exercises without enough context and infrastructure replicas remain discoverable through the full public GitHub profile.
- Desktop screenshot uses representative demonstration data. KahuPet and GALAX previews were captured from the actual public prototypes. The portrait is a centered vertical 3:4 crop of the user-selected linkedin_prof.jpg from Downloads; game art comes from Manuel's earlier public portfolio.
